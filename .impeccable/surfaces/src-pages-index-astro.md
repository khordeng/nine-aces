## Direction contract

THESIS: A club's own program, not a cafe flyer: the venue's name set like a team kit lettered on a jersey, then the house menu as a scannable price list. Refuses the category default of a photo-carousel hero with stacked feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Charcoal ground #0A0908 with warmer panel steps, champagne gold #D2B87D as the only accent (prices, CTAs, active rules, outline lettering), warm off-white text. Archivo expanded 800 for the name; Instrument Serif italic for headings and tagline; small tracked Archivo caps for labels. Flat surfaces, hairline rules, dotted leaders, 6/10px radii. No gradients, shadows, glow, blur.

STORY: Visitor learns this is Nine Aces, a pool hall and cafe with its own identity; sees where it is; scans real prices; taps WhatsApp to book.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left-aligned type stack filling the width: gold eyebrow (short address), NINE solid, ACES neutral outline, POOL & CAFE gold outline on one line, em-dash serif tagline, short profile, OPEN / SINCE / CONTACT info row, primary WhatsApp button + secondary See location. Fixed transparent navbar with gold logo on top.

FORM: brief-pinned editorial type-stack landing page. seed: none (pinned). Pinned by SPEC.md sections 3 and 4.2: "Brand reference: black and gold jersey (charcoal base, champagne gold metallic ornament, lotus/mandala motif). Overall feel: dark, elegant, editorial." and "large editorial type stack on near-black, small spaced caps above, italic serif tagline, small info row below."

Signature interaction: hero title lines rise out of clipped masks in sequence on load; navbar slides in, turns solid on scroll, hides on scroll down.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
